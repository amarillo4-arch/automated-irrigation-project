🌿 Development of an Automated Irrigation System

For Vegetable Plants at Brgy. Union, Mahaplag Elementary School

📌 Project Overview

This project aims to design and build an automated irrigation system that waters vegetable plants automatically — no manual watering needed. It helps save water, keeps plants healthy, and supports the school’s vegetable garden in Brgy. Union, Mahaplag, Leyte.

🎯 Objectives

  • Automatically water plants based on soil moisture level
  • Reduce manual effort and water waste
  • Improve growth of vegetables in the school garden
  • Provide a simple, affordable system for the school

⚙️ How It Works

The system uses a soil moisture sensor to check how wet the soil is:

  • ✅ Soil is dry → pump turns ON → water flows
  • ✅ Soil is wet enough → pump turns OFF → saves water
  • ✅ Can also be controlled manually

✨ Key Features

Auto Mode

Self-watering based on soil condition

Water Saving

Only waters when truly needed

Easy to Use

Simple setup, low maintenance

School-Friendly

Affordable & safe for students
